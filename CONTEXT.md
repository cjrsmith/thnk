# thnk

A calm spatial memory. Everything you think about is captured as a Thought and takes its
place in a single space whose shape is determined by when you thought it, so the structure
of your thinking becomes visible rather than filed away.

## Language

### The primitive

**Thought**:
The single primitive. Anything captured — an idea, a film to watch, a question, a task, a
fragment of a conversation. It has content and a creation time from the moment it exists;
everything else is optional and can be added later.
_Avoid_: Note, card, item, block, task, entry

**Label**:
A tag describing what is _in_ a Thought. A Thought carries zero or more. Labels are the
material for search and filtering; they are not expressed as position.
_Avoid_: Tag, keyword, category

**Topic**:
The single thing a Thought is _about_. At most one per Thought, which is what makes it
safe to express visually where Labels are not.
_Avoid_: Project, context, group, primary label

### The mind space

**Mind Space**:
The one raw space containing every Thought. It is the app's home and its primary
interface — not a view of the data but the representation of it.
_Avoid_: Galaxy, world, canvas, graph, universe

**The Deep**:
The dense centre of the Mind Space that Thoughts drift toward as they age. Old thinking
compresses here; recent thinking occupies the sparse outer rim.
_Avoid_: Black hole, nucleus, centre, core (reserved for the architectural core)

**Drift**:
The continuous inward movement of Thoughts toward the Deep over time. Both noun and verb:
a Thought drifts; its drift is a function of its age.
_Avoid_: Decay, aging, sinking

**Position**:
Where a Thought sits in the Mind Space. It is *computed* from creation time, never stored
and never manually set, so the Mind Space is identical wherever it is rendered.
_Avoid_: Coordinates, location, placement (which means something else)

### Arrangements

**Frame**:
A named, saved arrangement of Thoughts selected out of the Mind Space — a frame of mind.
Where the Mind Space is chronological and computed, a Frame is planar and arranged by
hand. The same Thought may appear in many Frames without being copied.
_Avoid_: View, layer, workspace, board, lens

**Placement**:
A Thought's stored position within one Frame. Placements exist only in Frames; the Mind
Space has none.
_Avoid_: Position (which is computed), coordinates

**Cluster**:
A labelled region drawn inside a Frame that gathers Thoughts placed within it. A grouping
made by hand for the purposes of one Frame, carrying no meaning outside it.
_Avoid_: Box, group, container, frame
